Rainbow Star Transport Ltd. was built on a simple idea: shippers deserve a carrier that drives safely, communicates honestly and shows up when it says it will.

Our story

Every colour of the rainbow. One standard.

Our logo is a star carrying a rainbow — every colour moving in the same direction. That's how we see logistics: many kinds of freight, many kinds of customers, all moving forward under one standard of care.

From full truckloads in dry vans, reefers and flatbeds to LTL, cross-docking, warehousing, distribution, storage and express freight, Rainbow Star Transport gives growing businesses one dependable partner instead of a patchwork of vendors. We keep things simple: clear quotes, honest timelines, and a real person on the phone when you need an update.

Our safety commitment

Safe driving starts long before the wheels turn.

Safety isn't a department at Rainbow Star — it's how every trip is planned, driven and delivered.

Pre-trip inspections

Equipment is checked before every departure — tires, brakes, lights, reefer units and securement gear.

Proper load securement

Freight is blocked, braced, strapped and tarped to protect it — and everyone around it.

Weather-smart planning

We watch road and weather conditions and adjust routes and timing to keep drivers and cargo safe.

Rested, compliant drivers

Hours-of-service rules are respected — a rested driver is a safe driver.

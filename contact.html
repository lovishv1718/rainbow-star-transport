FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

What's the difference between FTL and LTL?

Full Truckload (FTL) means your freight gets a dedicated trailer and travels direct. Less Than Truckload (LTL) means your pallets share a trailer with other shipments, so you only pay for the space you use. If you're not sure which fits, call us and we'll recommend the most cost-effective option.

What kinds of trailers do you have?

We run dry vans for general freight, refrigerated (reefer) trailers for temperature-sensitive goods, and flatbeds for lumber, steel, machinery and other open-deck loads — available for both full truckload and LTL.

Can you store my freight before delivery?

Yes. We offer both short-term storage (days to weeks) and long-term storage (months or more), plus warehousing, cross-docking and distribution — so your freight can be stored, staged and delivered by one team.

Do you handle urgent or same-day shipments?

Our express service is built for time-critical freight. For the fastest response, call dispatch directly at 709-699-1377 — any time, day or night.

What information do you need for a quote?

Pickup and delivery locations, ready date, what you're shipping, approximate weight or pallet count, and any special requirements like temperature control, tarping or appointment times. The quote form above covers everything.

How will I know where my shipment is?

We keep you updated proactively from pickup to delivery, and you can call or email us any time for a status check. Proof of delivery is sent once your freight arrives.
